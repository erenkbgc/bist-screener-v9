## 2. Synthesis: Why Plain `value_sn` Can Be Improved (and What Already Failed)

1. **Already-failed families (excluded from proposals):** `learned IC weights`, `dividend yield`, `foreign ownership`, `KAP red flags/timing/buybacks`, `size/liquidity filters`, `stops/exit buffers`, `entry timing`, `short-term rules`, `ST-debt coverage`, `EV variants`, `E/P ex deferred tax`, `tangible B/M`, `S1/M2/FV1`, `FF1`, `macro/event timing`, `H1a..H5/H8` (50/50 `gpa`, 50/50 `sue`, `f7<=2`, `chs`, `altman_em`, `mom_12_1` exclusion, `hi52` exclusion, `vol60` exclusion), `QV1–QV3 / LV1–LV2`.
2. **Three converging gaps in `value_sn` identified by the literature:**
   - **Gap A — Accounting Accrual & Inflation Bloat in Earnings (`Ball et al. 2016`; `Hirshleifer et al. 2004`; `Özkan & Kayalı 2015`; `Grecco et al. 2023`):** `value_sn` uses `ep` and `ey` based on accounting earnings (`ttm_ni`, `ttm_op_profit`), which are inflated by working-capital/operating-asset accruals (`ΔNOA`). In high inflation, nominal working-capital expansion looks like asset growth/profitability when cash generation is weak. Subtracting `ΔNOA` from operating profit (`CbOP`) cleanly separates cash-generating value from accrual traps using exact columns already in `quarterly.parquet`.
   - **Gap B — Behavioral Underreaction to Fundamental Surprises (`Atılgan 2026`; `Ahlatcıoğlu & Okay 2021`; `Novy-Marx 2015`):** `SUE` is the #1 surviving anomaly in BIST after multiple-testing adjustment (`|t| > 2.78`), yet `H5` failed because it gave `sue` an aggressive 50% weight, used nominal TL YoY changes (distorted by inflation drift), and ignored 22% missingness. Translating quarterly net income to USD (`q_ni / usdtry_q`) before taking YoY changes (`sue_ni_usd`) eliminates inflation drift and pairs cleanly as a modest 20% tilt on `value_sn` (`corr = +0.070`).
   - **Gap C — Joint High-Inflation Quality + Fundamental Momentum (`Ball et al. 2016` + `Atılgan 2026` + `Konchitchki 2011`):** Putting `NOA_{P-4}` into period-`P` CPI purchasing power (`cbop_real`) ensures dimensional consistency across both pre-IAS 29 and IAS 29 regimes, and combining `0.75 * value_sn_z + 0.15 * cbop_real_z_sn + 0.10 * sue_ni_usd_z_sn` unites cheapness, inflation-adjusted cash profitability, and hard-currency earnings momentum.
