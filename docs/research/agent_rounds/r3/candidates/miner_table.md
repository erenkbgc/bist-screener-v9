## Executive Synthesis: Four Distinct Complementary Signal Clusters

1. **Quarterly Operating & Gross Profit Surprise Yield (`m_qop_surp_mcap`, `m_qgp_surp_mcap`, `q_qop_chg_ta`, `d4q_qop_ta`, `q_sue_op`):**
   - Strongest short-to-medium horizon (`1m–3m`) orthogonal alpha in the entire dataset (`resid_v IC_3m` up to **`+0.0556, t = +5.91`**; `Long Q5-U` **`+11.1%` to `+14.9%/yr, t = +4.5 to +5.3`**; **8/8 calendar years positive**; `corr vs R0 = +0.07 to +0.14`).

   - Unlike old bottom-line `sue` (78% coverage, decays to zero by 12m: `IC_12m = +0.0082, t = 0.26`), operating and gross profit surprise yields achieve **98.5% coverage** and persist through 12m (`IC_12m = +0.0470, t = +2.96`).
2. **Cash-Based Operating Profitability & Enterprise Cash Operating Yield (`m_ocf_proxy_ev`, `q_ocf_proxy_ta`, `-q_d_noa_ta`, plus CPI-real `ocf_ev_real`):**
   - Strongest **zero-decay** orthogonal quality/cash-flow signal (`IC` rises monotonically from `1m` to `12m`: CPI-real `(ttm_op_profit - Δ_real NOA) / EV` posts `IC_1m = +0.0388 (t=+4.94)`, `IC_3m = +0.0537 (t=+5.31)`, `IC_6m = +0.0709 (t=+4.66)`, **`IC_12m = +0.0891 (t=+6.70)`**, **8/8 years positive**, `corr vs R0 = +0.19`).
   - Independently converges with `r3-scholar`'s Ball et al. (2016) `CbOP` literature review (`corr = +0.91`), while scaling by `EV` instead of `TA` turns it into a direct cash-flow valuation yield with stronger discovery `pb.simulate` lift (`D_lag75 = +3.49%/yr, t = +2.38`, `D_kap = +2.31%/yr, t = +1.68`, turnover `+0.7 pp`).
3. **Gross Profit Yield (`m_gp_mcap = ttm_gross_profit / mcap`, `m_qgp_mcap = 4 * q_gross_profit / mcap`):**
   - **#1 strongest 12-month orthogonal alpha across all 674 mined series** (`m_gp_mcap resid_v IC_12m = +0.0861, NW t = +8.80`; `m_qgp_mcap resid_v IC_12m = +0.0832, NW t = +7.34`; standalone `m_gp_mcap IC_12m = +0.1287, t = +8.56`).
   - Lowers portfolio turnover below `R0` (`20.2%` vs `21.4%`), has near-zero correlation with `scholar_cbop_real` (`+0.07`) and `scholar_sue_usd` (`-0.02`), and pairs powerfully with real operating profit surprise (`corr = +0.07` between the two legs).
4. **Intra-Year Earnings Acceleration (`q_ni_accel`, `q_op_accel`):**
   - Zero/slightly negative correlation with `value_sn_z` (`-0.05`), strong conditional IC inside top-40% value (`+0.0390, t = +3.06`, 8/8 yrs), but higher turnover (`24.5–28.1%`) caused weak net costed `pb.simulate` lift (`D_lag75 = +0.54%/yr, t = 0.44`; `D_kap = -0.22%/yr`), so it is **not** proposed as a standalone candidate.
